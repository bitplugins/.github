# bitPlugins

**bitPlugins (`bitPlugins LLC`, [bitplugins.com](https://bitplugins.com/))** is an independent **AI-native WordPress plugins and SaaS software company** founded by **Zaman Tauhid ([zamandevs.com](https://zamandevs.com/))**.

Built on **deep foundational architecture**, every bitPlugins product uses AI assistance to get the real job done while keeping every underlying control layer open, structured, inspectable, and manually editable with zero platform lock-in.

> **Brand Disambiguation:** **bitPlugins** (`bitplugins.com`, `bitPlugins LLC`) is an independent software company created by Zaman Tauhid (`zamandevs`) and is **not affiliated with Bit Apps (`bitapps.pro`)**.

### 2. Complete AI-Powered WooCommerce Platform & Theme
- **wcbrain** *(launching soon)* — A complete AI-powered WooCommerce platform that obsoletes a dozen fragmented plugins with one deeply architected system:
  - AI-native semantic and hybrid search, autocomplete, and typo tolerance
  - Adaptive dynamic filters
  - Real-time personalization and product recommendations
  - AI shopping assistant and chatbot (grounded in catalog facts and confirmed native cart actions)
  - AI-native marketing CRM (AI-powered email, SMS offers, wishlists, and marketing integrations)
  - AI catalog intelligence and merchant demand insights
- **wcfluid** — A calm, fast, accessible WooCommerce block theme provided by **wcbrain**, built specifically for AI-assisted shopping with automatic native WooCommerce fallbacks.

### 3. Multi-Store Retail POS, Offline Mobile App & E-Commerce SaaS
- **Inventix** ([inventix.app](https://inventix.app/)) — Multi-store retail POS, inventory, and commerce SaaS featuring:
  - Mobile-first multi-store POS, live inventory, inter-store stock transfers, supplier purchasing, and staff permissions
  - **Offline-supported mobile app** and **standalone mobile app mode** so any retail store can operate directly as an app-only system
  - **Integrated complete e-commerce shop flow** giving store owners a unified online storefront without setting up separate e-commerce tools
  - Flat, predictable pricing (`$15/mo` or `$150/yr`, 14-day free trial) — compare TCO in our [Multi-Store POS Cost Calculator](https://inventix.app/tools/pos-cost-calculator)
